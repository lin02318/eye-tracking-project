## We are building a prototype of an eye-tracking system used on lab animals

An eye-tracking system is used to study the behavior of animals. 

Traditional eye-tracking systems use cameras to capture the eye movement. It's big and heavy, and the video data bandwidth is too large, so it's hard to make it wireless. 

### There is another possible approach

Attach a tiny magnet on the surface of the eye, and fix a magnetic sensor on the animal's head. 

The magnetic sensor will measure the change in magnetic field, and hopefully be able to calculate the eye orientation. 

### Our goal is to build a prototype and verify if it's possible to track the eye movement from measuring the magnetic field

We attach a small magnet on a pin-pong ball as the eye model.  
We have a bracket to hold the ping-pong ball.  
The ping-pong ball is placed right above the magnetic sensor.  
There are angle markings on the bracket, so that we can adjust the angle of the ping-pong ball and compare it with the calculated angle from the sensor data.
